AutoFrac-Research is a C++ software developed during my PhD for the conception and study of fractal structures.

It provides tools for designing fractals from their boundaries and from polyhedron circle packings, as well as tools for measuring fractal dimension, area, perimeter, density, and persistent homology. The software can export fractal topologies for visualization with the MODITERE fractal modeler.
