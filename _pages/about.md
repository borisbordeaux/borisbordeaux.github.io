I am a postdoctoral researcher at Cornell Tech, working in the Matter of Tech Lab, co-supervised by [Gershon Elber](https://gershonelber.org/) and [Thijs Roumen](https://thijsroumen.eu/).

My research focuses on the design of lightweight structures using stress-based volumetric representations, with the goal of reducing material usage, weight, and fabrication time through computational design and 3D printing technologies. I am also exploring the design of multi-stream fractal heat exchangers using volumetric representations.

I am passionate about making complex geometric design more accessible. By developing intuitive and automated design methods, I aim to enable non-experts to work with and benefit from advanced computational design and digital fabrication technologies.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Université Bourgogne Europe (UBE) in 2025. My doctoral research focused on the automatic design of lacunar fractal structures. I worked on simplifying and extending the Boundary-Controlled Iterated Function System (BC-IFS) model, providing a more accessible approach to designing fractal structures while guaranteeing topological consistency.

My broader research interests include geometric modeling, computer-aided design, computer graphics, computational geometry, and digital fabrication.
